Campus Life

With a beautiful location,large campus and wide ranging facilities,we are able to
create robust and fun camps.For groups coming together and looking for a
customized program,we can create something to fit the needs of each group.Our
classes can range from high school,ESL or college courses combined with a variety
of activities (beach,on campus games,trips,volunteer opportunities,etc.).

For students coming alone,we work closely with the college to add international
students to weekly camps.This will be the ultimate opportunity for students to meet
Americans and experience a summer of a lifetime.Camps are based on themes for
an entire week (Harry Potter,robotics,surfing,etc.)and students can enroll in one or
more.
For more information,please email us at camps@tam.edu
