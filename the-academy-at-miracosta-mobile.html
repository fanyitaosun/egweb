Our Story

Blended High School & College

Located on the campus of MiraCosta College, The Academy combines small-group high school classes with college coursework. Students earn credits for both high school graduation and future university transfer while being fully immersed in campus life.

From day one, our faculty provide close academic support, ensuring students thrive in a new learning environment and understand the expectations of U.S. higher education.

Individualized Support

Personal Education Plans

During admissions we evaluate each student’s background, strengths, and ambitions. Together with academic counselors we craft an Individualized Education Plan that aligns course selection, tutoring, and support services with long-term goals.

Beyond academics, we coordinate housing, health insurance, and cultural activities so students enjoy a safe and balanced experience.

Campus Integration

Every student receives a MiraCosta College ID with full access to facilities, student clubs, and academic resources such as writing and math centers, the health clinic, and more.

Proudly Accredited

MSA-CESS Accreditation

The Academy at MiraCosta is regionally accredited by the Middle States Association Commission on Elementary and Secondary Schools (MSA-CESS), ensuring rigorous academic standards and global recognition.

Life at The Academy

Students balance challenging coursework with vibrant campus experiences, backed by teachers and staff who know them personally.
