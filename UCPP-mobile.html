Who Thrives in UCPP

Designed for ambitious students ready to experience authentic North American high school and college life while staying on track for top universities.

Ideal Candidates

  • High school students preparing for competitive university admission.
  • International learners seeking a fast-track to top North American universities.
  • Students excited about a dual-credit system and immersive campus experience.
  • Families eager to save time and education costs without compromising quality.

Why Students Choose UCPP

Program Advantages

  • Accelerated timeline: Complete middle school, high school, and the first year of university in just five years.
  • Dual credits: Every course counts toward both your high school diploma and your university transcript.
  • Guaranteed admission: Meet GPA and credit milestones to secure offers from partner North American universities.
  • Cost efficiency: Save up to 70% compared to traditional international education pathways.
  • Real U.S. campus life: Learn directly on the MiraCosta College campus with full student privileges.
