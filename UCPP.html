University Credits Pathway Program (UCPP)

The University Credits Pathway Program (UCPP) is a dual-credit program designed by The Academy at MiraCosta to fast-track students towards top North American universities. Experience real American high school and university life while earning both high school and university credits.

Who is UCPP For?

  • High school students preparing for university admission
  • International students aiming for a fast-track pathway to top North American universities
  • Students interested in a dual-credit system that offers both high school and university experience
  • Students looking to save time and money on traditional education paths

Why Choose UCPP?

  • Accelerated Timeline: Complete 8 years of high school and university education in just 5 years.
  • Dual Credit System: Earn both high school and university credits simultaneously, opening doors to top universities.
  • Guaranteed Admission: Meet GPA and credit requirements to guarantee admission to top North American universities.
  • Cost-Efficiency: Save up to 60-70% of education costs compared to traditional education paths.
  • Real American Experience: Study directly on an American campus with immersive learning opportunities.
