Activities & Services

Making every semester unforgettable

A Welcoming Start

Orientation Week

New arrivals dive into a week-long experience filled with campus tours, beach outings, and practical setup. We help students secure bank accounts, phones, and class schedules while forging friendships through games and community events.

Monthly Highlights

Community Traditions

Campus clubs and Academy-hosted events keep students engaged year-round. Expect potlucks, beach parties, game days, cultural celebrations, and more opportunities to share your story with classmates from around the globe.

On-Campus Resources

Students access the full suite of MiraCosta College services—from athletic facilities and clubs to tutoring centers and wellness support—ensuring a balanced lifestyle.

Signature Trips

Explore California & Beyond

Our team organizes excursions to Disneyland, Universal Studios, the San Diego Zoo, overnight camping adventures, and multi-campus tours across California—and sometimes other states.

Graduating seniors can even celebrate with the classic Disneyland Grad Night experience.
