Students interested in UC campuses may be placed on the Transfer Admission Guarantee (TAG) pathway. Meet the GPA and course requirements to secure guaranteed admission to participating universities.

Associate Degree for Transfer

California State University Pathway

The Associate Degree for Transfer (ADT) ensures your MiraCosta credits align with CSU requirements. Counselors keep you on track for a seamless move to campuses such as California State University San Marcos.
