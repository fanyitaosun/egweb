THE ACADEMY

The Academy is a uniquely blended program where high school students take courses to earn both high school and college credit at the same time. By earning college credits while completing high school, students have the opportunity to save not just time but also money.

Campus Life

As a student at The Academy, you get access to a wealth of extra-curricular activities and resources that make your experience unforgettable.

Junior Programs

With a beautiful location, large campus, and wide-ranging facilities, we partner with students on unique projects and camps that are always popular.
